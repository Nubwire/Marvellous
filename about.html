About Marvellous

We started Marvellous Entertainment because the films we loved were getting smaller releases every year, and the rooms showing them were closing. So we took on both ends: the cinemas and the releasing.

What we actually do

Three things, and they feed each other. We programme and run our own cinemas. We license films and release them theatrically across Australia. And we build the nights around them — premieres, Q&As, festival takeovers, score-live screenings.

Owning screens means we can back a film we believe in for longer than a single weekend. Releasing films means our cinemas get to show things nobody else has. That is the whole strategy.

How we programme

  • AUSTRALIAN FIRST

    Local films get a real run

    At least a third of our screen time goes to Australian and New Zealand work, and we hold films that find an audience rather than rotating them out on schedule.

  • REPERTORY

    Old films on the right format

    Monday classics and Sunday matinees, from a print where we can get one. $6 for members, because the point is that people come.

  • EVENTS

    Reasons to leave the house

    Filmmakers in the room, festival seasons, one-night-only shorts programmes and the occasional live score.

For filmmakers and sales agents

We acquire Australian and international features for theatrical release in Australia, and we programme one-off screenings all year. If you have a finished film, a rough cut or a festival premiere coming up, we would like to see it.

Send a link and a password to acquisitions@marvellousentertainment.com.au with the running time, format and any festival history. We answer everything, usually within three weeks.

Working with us

We hire projectionists, programmers, front of house and bar staff at every cinema, and we would rather train someone who loves films than poach someone who does not.

Open roles are listed on the contact page. If nothing is listed, send us a note anyway.

Membership, in full

$99 a year, or $79 concession. Cancel any time and we refund the unused months.

What you get

Booking opens 48 hours early on every session including premieres, one guest pass a month, $6 Monday classics, member pricing at the bar, and first refusal on festival passes.

Where it works

All three cinemas, and West End when it opens. Guest passes are transferable; membership is not.

How to join

At any box office, or through the contact form while online joining is being built.
