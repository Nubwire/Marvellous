Our cinemas

Three open, one on the way. Every room is wheelchair accessible, hearing loop equipped, and runs captioned and audio described sessions each week.

Marvellous Newtown

King Street, Newtown NSW 2042

Three screens behind a 1920s shopfront. The main room seats 180 under a barrel ceiling we spent a year getting the acoustics right in. The bar opens an hour before the first evening session and stays open after the last one.

Open 10am until late, seven days.
Getting here Six minutes from Newtown station. No car park — street parking is metered until 10pm.

See Newtown sessions

  • ACCESS

    Step-free throughout

    Lift to all three screens, companion seating in every row block, and Companion Card holders come free.

  • SENSORY SESSIONS

    Second Sunday, monthly

    Lights up, volume down, free movement in and out of the room. All ages welcome.

Marvellous Fitzroy

Smith Street, Fitzroy VIC 3065

Four screens upstairs, and a rooftop that runs deck chair screenings from November to March. Screen 3 is our smallest at 42 seats and our favourite — it is where most Q&As happen.

Open 11am until late, seven days.
Getting here Tram 86 stops at the door. Bike parking in the laneway.

See Fitzroy sessions

Marvellous Fremantle

High Street, Fremantle WA 6160

A restored 1930s hall with two screens, original plasterwork and a working 35mm projector. Sunday matinees run from a print whenever we can get one.

Open 12pm until late, Wednesday to Sunday.
Getting here Ten minutes from Fremantle station, next to the Esplanade car park.

See Fremantle sessions

Marvellous West End, opening 2027

Boundary Street, West End QLD 4101

Three screens and a courtyard bar in a former printworks. Fitout starts this spring; we will open with a week of free screenings for the neighbourhood before ticketed sessions begin.

Good to know

How early should I arrive?

Doors open 15 minutes before the advertised time. Trailers run for about eight minutes, so the feature starts roughly ten past.

Can I bring children?

Yes, within the film's classification. Under 15s need an adult with them for MA15+ sessions. Babies at the Movies runs weekly at Fitzroy.

Do you run captioned and audio described sessions?

Every cinema runs at least two captioned sessions and one audio described session each week. They are marked on the programme, and headsets are at the box office.

What about refunds?

Exchange or refund up to two hours before your session, through the link in your confirmation email or at the box office.

Can I hire a cinema?

Yes — whole rooms for birthdays, screenings, launches and staff nights. Details on the contact page.
